## The one proposal

**Losses to ranked opponents, by cause** (from `analyse_losses.py 56173004`, the
product whose net revenue swung the game):

```
soraemon        #23   WHEAT +4,021
Arda Ceylan     #126  EGG   +4,181
kta_jpn         #261  WHEAT +3,387
Gobi Elangovan  #322  EGG   +2,764
JALKARNA GAUTAM #404  WHEAT +5,253
gradfield21     #428  WHEAT +2,656
today           #440  EGG   +3,924   (and, second game) WOOL +21,214
DI27 RF         #491  WOOL  +28,331
MKGA            #623  MILK  +8,831
```

WHEAT is the single largest and most frequent cause, both overall (17 of 57 losses,
$158,148 total gap — the largest of any product) and specifically among the 10 losses
to ranked opponents (4 of 10). Checked the naive theories first and ruled them out:
"day-0 melon" opener is in 94% of *wins* too (not discriminating), sheep-heavy /
cow-heavy opponent builds are not over-represented in losses either, and a seat-order
pricing theory (player 0 quoted first) is contradicted by the engine source itself —
`_process_market` quotes both players off the *same* pre-commit inventory each
lockstep unit, so same-turn same-quantity orders get the same price.

What actually explains it, traced through the instrumented ledger on every WHEAT-cause
loss checked (soraemon, kta_jpn, JALKARNA GAUTAM, gradfield21, plus one unranked
control): our schedule consistently plants **less wheat and more carrot** than the
opponents beating us on this axis, with *identical total actions and identical hands* —
this is a crop-mix choice, not idle capacity:

```
                        us wheat/carrot   them wheat/carrot
soraemon      (#23)        157 / 37          163 / 31
kta_jpn       (#261)       144 / 53          164 / 31
JALKARNA GAUTAM (#404)     144 / 53          188 /  9
gradfield21   (#428)       157 / 37          163 / 31
(unranked control)         144 / 53          188 /  9
```

Wheat is consumed by 5 of the 8 shops (BAKERY, BRUNCH_SPOT, FARMERS_MARKET,
ICE_CREAM_SHOP, PIZZA_SHOP); carrot only by 2 (PET_CAFE, FARMERS_MARKET), and
PET_CAFE is the shallowest sink in the game per the shop-drain arithmetic above.
Consistently over-planting carrot relative to that mix risks selling into a shallow,
easily-saturated market while leaving broader wheat demand unclaimed.

**Proposal:** shift some of the schedule's carrot tile-turns to wheat, aimed at
closing the wheat gap specifically (not a general "grow more" change — total actions
would stay the same). This is a hypothesis, not yet built or tested — no candidate
implementing it exists yet.

**What would falsify it:** build the rebalanced schedule as a candidate, veto-test it
against the live incumbent (`candidate_v4.py`) on a fresh unused seed block, both
seats, per `/schedule`'s rule. If it does not beat the incumbent outright, or if it
wins the pool but loses the veto, or if `analyse_losses.py` on the new candidate still
shows WHEAT as a leading cause of loss on held-out ranked-opponent replays, the theory
is wrong and should be dropped rather than patched.
