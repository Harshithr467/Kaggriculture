## The one proposal, tested

**Losses to ranked opponents, by cause** (`analyse_losses.py 56195164`, 97 losses on
disk today, up from 57 on 09-12):

```
WHEAT        primary cause in 43 of 97 losses; total gap $292,085
MILK         primary cause in 19 of 97 losses; total gap $145,294
WOOL         primary cause in 14 of 97 losses; total gap $179,140
TOMATO       primary cause in  7 of 97 losses; total gap $122,102
EGG          primary cause in  7 of 97 losses; total gap $65,421
STRAWBERRY   primary cause in  5 of 97 losses; total gap $95,135
CARROT       primary cause in  2 of 97 losses; total gap $27,476
```

Same finding as 09-12 (then: WHEAT 17/57, $158,148), now on almost double the sample:
WHEAT is still by far the largest and most frequent cause. The 09-12 report traced
this to a crop-mix gap -- with identical total actions and identical hands, we plant
157 WHEAT / 37 CARROT across the season where opponents beating us on this axis plant
163-188 WHEAT / 9-31 CARROT.

**Proposal:** swap the route's late-season carrot plantings toward wheat to match the
163/31 ratio measured on the two most-studied cases (soraemon #23, gradfield21 #428)
exactly, rather than guessing at a number. Built as `kernels/candidate_v6.py`: a
`WHEAT_SWAP` patch (mirrors the existing `CARROT_SWAP` mechanism already in
`agent_combined.py`, run in the opposite direction) that flips 6 of the route's 37
carrot `PLANT` ops to wheat, picked from the early/middle of the only window carrot
appears in (steps 609-665, the last ~5 in-game days) so the swapped tiles still clear
wheat's slower `max_yield_day` (4, vs. carrot's 3) before the season ends at step 719.
Confirmed by decoding the route that this moves routes 0-3 to exactly 163 WHEAT / 31
CARROT. `_trim_seed_buffer` already rescans the tape for future `PLANT` ops each turn,
so seed purchases adjust automatically -- no other hand-edits needed.

**Falsifier and result:** veto against the live incumbent (`candidate_v5.py`), fresh
seed block `94000-94039` (first use, recorded in `/schedule`'s ledger), both seats:

```
python ladder.py --pool "kernels/candidate_v6.py#V6" "kernels/candidate_v5.py#V5" \
    --seeds 94000-94039 --workers 20
```

```
agent   W-L      win%      BT
V5      80-0     100.0%    3360
V6       0-80      0.0%    -360

head to head: V6 0-80 V5, mean margin -1,281
```

**Rejected.** A clean sweep, not a close call -- the veto is doing exactly what it is
there for. The crop-mix correlation from the loss analysis does not transfer by
directly copying the ratio: matching winning opponents' wheat/carrot *quantities*
made us worse, not better. Likely explanation checked against `kaggriculture.py`'s own
market params: CARROT has the steepest scarcity curve of any product in the game
(`below_func: hinge`, `below_target: 1.00`, vs. wheat's `sqrt`/`0.80`) and -- per the
project's own older `CARROT_SWAP` note -- carrot is already leaguewide undersupplied
because route-clone agents don't grow it, so its price runs persistently hot. Trading
scarce, steep-curve carrot for common, flatter-curve wheat gives up high-margin
revenue for low-margin revenue even though it superficially matches what winning
opponents' planting counts look like -- the correlation in the loss data is real but
is not simply "plant what they plant." `candidate_v6.py` is kept in `kernels/` as a
documented, tested, rejected ablation, same convention as `candidate_v4_nostep0.py`.
Not submitting. WHEAT stays the open problem; the next attempt on it should look at
*when* and *at what price* opponents sell wheat/carrot rather than how much they plant.
